# Product Roadmap

## v0.1 Foundation
Application shell, schema, seed data, module navigation, local infrastructure.

## v0.2 Master Data
Database-backed product, variant, brand, category, warehouse and supplier CRUD. Excel import validation.

## v0.3 Sales and Inventory
Sales import, inventory transactions, stock balances, ageing, sell-through and margin analytics.

## v0.4 Purchasing
Purchase order workflow, approvals, receiving, in-transit stock and supplier performance.

## v0.5 Forecasting
Baseline models, backtesting, MAPE/WAPE, seasonality and forecast by model/color/storage.

## v0.6 Supply Planning
Safety stock, reorder point, stockout date, recommended order quantity, cash constraints and scenarios.

## v0.7 Commercial AI
Natural-language analysis, management briefs, purchase recommendations, exceptions and alerts.

## v1.0 Production
Authentication, audit logs, security hardening, backups, monitoring, ERPNext integration and deployment.
